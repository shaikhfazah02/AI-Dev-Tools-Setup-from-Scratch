## Post 2
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/richardvanderblom_this-morning-i-went-to-my-local-bakery-here-activity-7470379563026661379-wwIy

This morning I went to my local bakery here in Valencia.
I tried to sell them my own bread. They were confused, looked at me like I was insane.

Yet every single week I receive multiple DM's doing exactly the same.

"Hi Richard, I enjoyed your recent post. Would you be interested in setting up a 30-min call on how we can improve your LinkedIn social selling initiatives?"

Three things are wrong with this.

1. You didn't read my post. You scanned it.
2. You have no idea what I need. You just know what you're selling.
3. You're pitching social selling services. To somebody who's been into social selling for 20 years

I always ask the same question: "Are you the guy that walks into a bakery selling his own bread?"
Funny enough. They never respond.

I can smell bad outreach from a mile distance.

→ Generic opener that could go to anyone
→ Immediate pitch before any connection
→ Zero research on who you're talking to
→ Selling a service you clearly don't use yourself

You want to know what works for me and my clients?

→ Reference something specific/ real insight from their content
→ Ask a genuine question before you pitch anything
→ Show you understand their world before offering yours
→ Lead with curiosity. Not a calendar link.

The difference between bad and good outreach takes 5 more minutes of your time.

Lazy outreach treats people as targets.
Good outreach treats people as humans.

One gets ignored. The other starts conversations.
